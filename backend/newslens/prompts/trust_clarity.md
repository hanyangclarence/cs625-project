Score the **clarity** of one news article on a 0–30 scale.

Clarity = whether the article makes its central claims, sourcing, and timeline understandable to a non-expert reader.

Anchors:
- 25–30: well-organized, clear lede, attributions are easy to follow, technical terms explained.
- 15–24: readable but some unsupported jargon, mixed framing, or buried lede.
- 5–14: confusing structure, unclear attribution, or interpretive content presented as fact.
- 0–4: largely opinion, unparseable, or so short there's nothing to assess.

Output a JSON object: `{"clarity": int, "rationale": "one short sentence"}`. Output JSON only.
